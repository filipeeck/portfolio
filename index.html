About me

A short version of a long story.

I've spent the past several years designing products across very different worlds — auditing tools used in factory floors, tax software for accountants, Web3 onboarding flows, AI-first email. The common thread: making the complicated feel obvious.

I move comfortably between strategy and craft. One day that means leading a research synthesis with a PM and engineering lead; the next, it's pushing pixels on a component spec or pairing with a developer to ship a tricky interaction. I care about the work being good, not about who gets credit for it.

How I work

A process built around evidence, not opinion.

Every project gets some version of this — collapsed or expanded depending on scope, but the same shape.

What I focus on

Four things I keep coming back to.

User-centered, business-aware

Great design sits at the intersection of what users need and what the business needs to ship. I work hard to keep both in view.

Design systems that scale

Built and contributed to systems on Material, MUI, and Tailwind. I treat them as products — versioned, documented, maintained.

Cross-functional collaboration

Some of the best design decisions I've made started as engineering or product conversations. I work close, not behind a wall.

Accessibility as a floor

WCAG 2.1 AA is the starting point. Inclusive design isn't a checklist — it changes which solutions are even on the table.
